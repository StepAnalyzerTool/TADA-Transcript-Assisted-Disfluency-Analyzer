# DART counting rules

Occurrences per 100 lexical words = target occurrences / total lexical words spoken × 100.

Occurrences per minute = target occurrences / verified session duration in minutes.

Lexical targets remain in the denominator. Configured nonlexical vocalizations, timestamps, cue numbers, speaker labels, platform metadata, URLs, non-speech annotations, and speech from unselected speakers are excluded.
